WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

You are building software that helps a business manage customer information in one organised system.

A user will be able to create an account and store customer details such as:

•Name

•Phone number

•Email address

•Company

•Job title

•Address

•Location

•Notes

The software will also allow the user to search, filter, sort, update, and delete customer records.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Many small businesses do not have a reliable customer record system.

Customer information may be stored inside phone contacts, notebooks, messaging applications, spreadsheets, receipts, or the memory of one employee.

This creates several problems.

The business may struggle to find a customer’s number.

Two employees may create the same customer record.

Important notes may be forgotten.

Customer information may be lost when a phone is replaced.

The business may also find it difficult to understand how many customers it has or how quickly the customer list is growing.

A Customer Record Management System solves these problems by keeping customer information in one structured database.
