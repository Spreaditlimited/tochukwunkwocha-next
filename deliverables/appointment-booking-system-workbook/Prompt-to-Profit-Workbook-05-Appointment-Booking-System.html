UNDERSTANDING THE APPOINTMENT BOOKING SYSTEM

Estimated Time

WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING

You are building software that helps a business manage appointment information in one organised system.

A user will be able to create an account and store appointment details such as:

•Name

•Phone number

•Email address

•Service

•Appointment date and time

•Appointment Date

•Start and end time

•Notes

The software will also allow the user to search, filter, sort, update, and cancel appointments.

Every user’s appointment information will remain private.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Many small businesses do not have a reliable appointment system.

Appointment information may be stored inside phone contacts, notebooks, messaging applications, spreadsheets, receipts, or the memory of one employee.

This creates several problems.

The business may struggle to find an appointment’s number.

Two employees may create the same appointment.

Important notes may be forgotten.

Appointment information may be lost when a phone is replaced.

The business may also find it difficult to understand how many appointments it has or how quickly the appointment list is growing.

A Appointment Booking System solves these problems by keeping appointment information in one structured database.
